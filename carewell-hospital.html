FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Have a question? We probably have the answer. If not, our team is always happy to help.

How do I book an appointment?

You can book an appointment online through our website, by calling our helpline at 1800-CARE-911, or by walking into any of our branches. Same-day appointments are available for most specialties.

What insurance plans do you accept?

We are empanelled with all major insurance providers including Star Health, HDFC Ergo, Bajaj Allianz, New India Assurance, and many more. Our billing team can assist with cashless claims.

Are your health checkup packages customisable?

Yes, all our health checkup packages can be customised based on your age, medical history, and specific concerns. Our wellness counsellors will guide you to the right package.

How soon will I receive my test reports?

Most routine reports are available within 4–6 hours. Advanced tests like biopsies and cultures may take 24–72 hours. Reports are shared digitally and in person.

Do you have facilities for international patients?

Yes, our International Patient Services team handles visa letters, airport pickup, accommodation, translation support, and dedicated care coordinators for patients travelling from abroad.
